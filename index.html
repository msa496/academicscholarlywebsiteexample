About

My name is Margot and I am the STEM Librarian at Villanova University I am the liaison to seven departments in the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences. I maintain collections for each unique department and help with research needs including conducting information literacy sessions in classrooms.

Prior to joining Falvey Library, I earned my bachelor’s degree in Physics at Skidmore College, then went on to earn my master’s degree in Imaging Science at the Rochester Institute of Technology. My passion for library science came from working in the bibliographic services department at Skidmore’s Lucy Scribner Library during my undergraduate career. This led me to transition to library science through coursework and as a circulation assistant at the Phoenixville Public Library, while simultaneously working in the STEM industry.
